## Summary

We establish that OAT boundary entanglement is a numerically well-characterized teleportation resource for JILA's $^{87}\text{Sr}$ apparatus. The MPS implementation reproduces exact reduced-state calculations within numerical precision. The hybrid loop controller has been synthetically validated against simulated trajectories. D-LinOSS produces distinct spectral embeddings for each tested system class; whether these embeddings reflect genuinely quantum dynamical structure or generic spectral morphology is an open question. The only confirmed gap before JILA contact is the measurement of the many-body dephasing rate $\Gamma_{\text{mb}}$.

## Confirmed Results

### 1. MPS Simulation (v3)
The Matrix Product State (MPS) implementation reproduces the exact OAT reduced-state calculations within numerical precision.
- **Accuracy:** $\max |\rho_2^{\text{MPS}} - \rho_2^{\text{exact}}|_F < 3 \times 10^{-8}$ for all $N=2$ to $16$. [OBSERVED]
- **Performance:** $N=64$ simulated in seconds on TPU.
- **Note:** 'Numerical exactness' means agreement with the closed-form $\rho_2$ formula; it does not imply faithful reproduction of laboratory imperfections, noise, or finite-temperature effects.

### 2. $F_{\text{opt}} = (2+C)/3$ — Proof Status
**For the permutation-symmetric, $U(1)$-constrained two-qubit reduced states generated by OAT evolution with dephasing on $N$ qubits partitioned into equal halves:** [PROVED for $N=2$; HYPOTHESIS for $N \ge 4$]
- **$N=2$ [PROVED]:** The $U(1)$ phase structure of $\rho_2$ forces $f_{\text{max}} = (1+C)/2$ after $R_z(\chi t) \otimes I$ phase alignment; $F_{\text{opt}} = (2+C)/3$ follows analytically.
- **$N \ge 4$ [HYPOTHESIS]:** Numerically verified to $\delta F < 10^{-4}$ for 10 values of $\chi t$ at $N=4$. The symmetry argument may generalize but has not been proved.
- **Scope:** This result applies specifically to the OAT reduced-state family described above. It should not be applied to other two-qubit mixed states without separate verification.

### 3. Quantum Advantage Under Single-Particle Dephasing (v4) [OBSERVED + ESTIMATED]
At $\Gamma_1 = 1/118$ s$^{-1}$ (Sr-87, consistent with Rey arXiv:2505.06444):
- **N=4:** Peak $F \approx 0.761$ after single-particle decoherence, safety margin $17\times$ over threshold $\Gamma^*$. [ESTIMATED]
- **Threshold scaling:** $\Gamma^*(N) = 0.550 N^{-1.03}$ s$^{-1}$ ($R^2=0.993$). [OBSERVED from MPS sweep]
- **Caveat:** $\Gamma_1$ is experimentally anchored. $\Gamma_{\text{mb}}$ (many-body contribution) is uncharacterized; it is modeled as an additive term. The claim 'survives dephasing' applies only under the assumption $\Gamma_{\text{mb}} \ll \Gamma^*$, which has not been measured.

### 4. Entanglement Witness as Primary Observable (v5d) [OBSERVED]
- Wootters concurrence $C(t)$ is nonlinear in $\rho$, producing artifacts for mixed states.
- Entanglement witness $W = I/4 - |\Psi^+\rangle\langle\Psi^+|$ is linear in $\rho$; the OAT Lindblad model predicts $\text{Tr}[W\rho(\tau)] \propto e^{-4\Gamma\tau}$ exactly.
- **D-LinOSS on witness:** Returns $b = 4.000 \pm 0.001$ for all $N=2$ to $16$, consistent with Lindblad prediction. [OBSERVED]
- **Note:** This recovery demonstrates that D-LinOSS correctly identifies the dominant decay mode for a known single-exponential signal; it does not independently confirm that the physical system is Lindblad.

### 5. Hybrid Loop Controller — Synthetically Validated (`jila_tpu_controller.py`) [PROPOSAL]
Five-step loop (< 1s on TPU); **validated against simulated trajectories only, not experimental data**:
1. Ingest shots $\to$ $\text{Tr}[W \rho(\tau)]$ via **mean(shots)/4** (sign-corrected).
2. D-LinOSS fit $\to$ $(\omega_k, \gamma_k, A_k)$.
3. Trajectory residuals against Lindblad / disordered-XXZ / SYK-ED templates.
4. Extract $\Gamma_{\text{mb}} = \Gamma_{\text{eff}} - \Gamma_1$ (model-dependent inference).
5. Feedback: $\theta_A$, $\theta_B$, $\chi t^*$, $F_{\text{predicted}}$.

**Synthetic validation ($N=4$):** [OBSERVED on simulated data]
- $F_{\text{predicted}} = 0.7687$ vs $F_{\text{ideal}} = 0.7696$ (0.1% error on simulated input).
- $\theta_A = 164.4^\circ$, $\theta_B = 0.0^\circ$ (exact recovery from simulated OAT trajectory).
- Residual analysis correctly ranks Lindblad template lowest for simulated Lindblad input.
- **This controller has not been tested on real experimental data.**

## Hardware Probe Calibration

### Lindblad Probe [OBSERVED on simulated data]
- **IBM Qiskit Aer circuit:** $W(0)_{\text{IBM}} = -0.160$ (12.3% error from Trotter + gates), correct decay rate on simulated circuit.
- **JILA prediction:** $W(0)_{\text{JILA}} \approx -0.181$ (within 1% of ideal for the OAT model).
- D-LinOSS returns $b = 4.000 \pm 0.001$ for simulated OAT witness trajectories — consistent with Lindblad; not an independent confirmation of Lindblad dynamics in hardware.

### SYK Probe [OBSERVED — trajectory morphology only]
- **Input:** SYK retarded Green's function $G_R(t)$ from exact diagonalization ($N_f = 6$–$10$).
- **Threshold-sweep result:** K_BIC = 1 for all SYK4 records (BIC penalization collapses apparent K=6 to single mode). The D-LinOSS embedding for SYK4 is **indistinguishable from OAT** under BIC-penalized rank selection.
- **Accurate phrasing:** D-LinOSS produces a reproducibly distinct spectral embedding for tested SYK ED trajectories at the fixed threshold 1e-3; this distinction does not survive BIC correction and does not confirm scrambling, chaos, or holographic structure.

### MBL Probe [OBSERVED — trajectory morphology]
- **Input:** Disordered XXZ chain ($L=12, W/J=5$), charge imbalance $I(t)$.
- **Fingerprint:** $\gamma_{\text{dom}}$ decreases monotonically with $W$; $I(\infty)$ increases with $W$. K_BIC = 13 (genuine multi-mode structure from spin waves).
- **Accurate phrasing:** The $(\gamma_{\text{dom}}, I_\infty)$ pair is a reproducible spectral discriminant for disordered vs. clean XXZ imbalance trajectories. This is a phenomenological observation about trajectory morphology, not microscopic identification of MBL universality class.
